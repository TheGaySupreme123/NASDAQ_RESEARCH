On December 17, 2024, pursuant to a notice of exercise of 3,500,000 Warrant Shares from an investor dated December 16, 2024, the Company issued 8,872,792 Class A Ordinary Shares and Pre-Funded Warrants to purchase 12,140,447 shares of Class A Ordinary Shares.
