CEO PURCHASES SHARES OF MDXHEALTH

 

MDxHealth SA (NASDAQ: MDXH) (the “Company” or “mdxhealth”) announced today that on March 6, 2025, Michael McGarrity, Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board of Directors of the Company, informed the Company that he had purchased an aggregate of 50,000 of the Company’s ordinary shares for a total aggregate purchase price of approximately $80,000.

 

This information is being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for the purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing made by the Company under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as otherwise set forth herein or as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such a filing. Mr. McGarrity is not generally required to disclose his trading in the Company’s ordinary shares (other than in limited circumstances, including in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F) and does not assume any obligation to disclose any additional purchases or sales, except where required by law.
