Wearable Devices Ltd. (the “Company”) is filing this Report on Form 6-K to report an adjustment to the exercise price of its warrants to purchase ordinary shares of the Company that were issued in the Company’s initial public offering which closed on September 15, 2022 (the “Warrants”). Pursuant to Section 3(i) of the Warrants, effective after the closing of trading on December 14, 2022 (the 90th calendar day immediately following the issuance date of the Warrants), the Warrants were adjusted pursuant to their terms to reduce the exercise price of the Warrants to $2.00 per share.
