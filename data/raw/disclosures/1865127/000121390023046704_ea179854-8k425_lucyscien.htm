Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

On June 5, 2023, Lucy Scientific Discovery Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing it has submitted a non-binding proposal (the “Proposal”) to Pasithea Therapeutics Corp. (“Pasithea”) to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of its common stock. Under the Proposal, the Company would offer $0.60 in cash and $0.25 in the Company’s common stock for every share of Pasithea common stock, representing a value of $0.85 per share. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.
