EMPLOYEE COMMUNICATION

Subject: Important Vigil News Today

Team,

This evening we announced that Vigil is expected to be acquired by Sanofi. Through this transaction, we are establishing a strong path for the future of TREM2 for Alzheimer’s disease. Sanofi shares our confidence and passion for our small molecule TREM2 agonist program and its tremendous potential. We believe this next step will allow us to leverage their resources to further advance VG-3927 and ultimately reach and positively impact patients. We also believe this transaction will create significant benefits for all stakeholders. The press release we issued is available on our website here: https://investors.vigilneuro.com/news-events/news.

First and foremost, let me say that I am extremely proud of what our team has accomplished. This recognition by a global company like Sanofi is a testament to your efforts, and the incredible value we have created. Equally important, I want to acknowledge that this is a big announcement, and with that can sometimes come uncertainty around change. The executive team and I will be here to support you at every step of this process as we move towards officially becoming part of Sanofi. I know there will be many questions over the next few months, and we will do our best to answer them as decisions are made and we have information to share. For right now, we have included an FAQ document that will hopefully address some initial questions you may have.

We believe that Sanofi is the right fit for us, and this deal comes at the right time. As you know, we have worked diligently over the past five years to develop our innovative product candidates with the goal of bringing transformative therapies to patients and their families. We look forward to working with Sanofi to advance our small molecule program. As part of the transaction and following the Phase 2 IGNITE data readout, we will be returning VGL101 to Amgen. We believe this was the best next step for Vigil and our programs at this time, because it is expected to deliver substantial value to our stockholders and give our product candidates the best chance at success.

Looking at the bigger picture, this transaction closely aligns with Vigil’s steadfast mission to improve the lives of patients, caregivers and families affected by neurodegenerative diseases. Sanofi has a strategic focus on neurology and immunology, and recognizes our complementary expertise in these areas. We believe that this transaction will allow us to leverage Sanofi’s teams, broad platform and far-reaching network to potentially bring our important and differentiated therapy to patients suffering with Alzheimer’s disease–a devastating disease with significant unmet need.

Until the transaction closes, which we expect to happen in the third quarter of 2025, we will continue to operate as a separate, independent company. It is important that we conduct business as usual during this time and remain focused on our day-to-day responsibilities.

On behalf of the executive team, I want to thank each of you for your unrelenting dedication and commitment to Vigil. Together, I believe we have developed something extraordinary to positively impact patients, and again, I couldn’t be prouder. We appreciate your patience and flexibility during this transition.

We will be hosting an All-Company meeting at 7:30 a.m. tomorrow morning. Be on the lookout for a calendar invite shortly with the meeting details. I look forward to discussing today’s news with you soon.

Best,

Ivana
