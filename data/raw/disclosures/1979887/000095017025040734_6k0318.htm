RESIGNATION OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER

On March 17, 2025, Ms. Xiaoling Song tendered her resignation from her position as chief financial officer (“CFO”) of XCHG Limited (the “Company”) for personal reasons, effective as of April 11, 2025.

The Company has initiated a search for a permanent CFO with the necessary capabilities and qualifications. Mr. Yifei Hou, chief executive officer of the Company, will serve as the interim CFO and interim principal financial / accounting officer for the Company’s annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year of 2024 and will supervise the review of the upcoming unaudited financial results.
