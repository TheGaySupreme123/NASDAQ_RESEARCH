On August 19, 2025, Erayak Power Solution Group Inc (the “Company”), a Cayman Islands exempted company, consummated a share subscription (the “Share Subscription”) with Erayak International Limited, a British Virgin Islands company. Under the terms of the Share Subscription, the Erayak International Limited purchased 8,000,000 Class B ordinary shares from the Company at a consideration of $0.055 per share, which is the closing pricing on August 18, 2025. A copy of the Share Subscription letter is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1.

 

Erayak International Limited now controls 9,000,000 Class B ordinary shares of the Company, representing approximately 46.4% of the total issued and outstanding ordinary shares. Erayak International Limited holds no additional shares of the Company.
