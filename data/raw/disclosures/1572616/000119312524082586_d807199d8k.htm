Item 7.01

Regulation FD Disclosure.

On April 1, 2024, Fractyl Health, Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that the Company has received Investigational Device Exemption from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration to study Revita’s efficacy in maintaining weight loss following the discontinuation of GLP-1 receptor agonist (GLP-1RA) drug therapy. A copy of the press release in connection with the announcement is being furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.

The Company has posted an updated corporate slide presentation to reflect recent business and financial updates. The updated slide presentation can be found on the Company’s investor relations website https://ir.fractyl.com.

The information contained in Item 7.01 of this Current Report (including Exhibit 99.1 attached hereto) shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as expressly provided by specific reference in such a filing.
