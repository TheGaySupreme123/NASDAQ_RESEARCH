Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On June 9, 2025, Bicara Therapeutics Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2025 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). The stockholders considered two proposals at the Annual Meeting, each of which is described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 25, 2025. Of the 54,536,218 shares outstanding as of the record date, 47,737,314 shares or approximately 87%, were present or represented at the Annual Meeting. Set forth below are the results of the matters submitted for a vote of the stockholders at the Annual Meeting.

Proposal 1: Election of Directors

The Company's stockholders elected each of the following individuals to serve as class I directors for a three year term ending at the Company’s 2028 annual meeting of stockholders and until his or her successor has been duly elected and qualified, or until his or her earlier death, resignation or removal, with the votes cast as follows:
