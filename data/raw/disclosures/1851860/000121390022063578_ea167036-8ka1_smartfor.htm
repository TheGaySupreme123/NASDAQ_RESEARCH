EXPLANATORY NOTE

 

On July 29, 2022, Smart for Life Inc. (the “Company”) acquired all of the issued and outstanding membership interests of Ceautamed Worldwide, LLC (“Ceautamed”), pursuant to a securities purchase agreement, dated March 14, 2022, among the Company, RMB Industries, Inc., RTB Childrens Trust and D&D Hayes, LLC, as amended.

This Amendment No. 1 to Current Report on Form 8-K/A amends the Form 8-K that the Company filed on August 4, 2022 to include the financial statements of the business acquired as required by Items 9.01(a) and 9.01(b) of Form 8-K.

(a) Financial Statements of Business Acquired

 

The audited consolidated financial statements of Ceautamed for the year ended December 31, 2021 and the accompanying notes thereto are filed as Exhibit 99.1 attached hereto and are incorporated by reference herein.

 

The unaudited consolidated financial statements of Ceautamed for the six months ended June 30, 2022 and the accompanying notes thereto are filed as Exhibit 99.2 attached hereto and are incorporated by reference herein.

 

(b) Pro forma financial information

 

The unaudited pro forma combined financial information giving effect to the acquisition is filed as Exhibit 99.3 attached hereto and is incorporated herein by reference.
