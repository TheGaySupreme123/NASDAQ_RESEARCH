Explanatory Note

On April 17, 2025, Snow Lake Resources Ltd. (the "Company") distributed a meeting circular and certain other related materials to its shareholders of record as of March 31, 2025 (the "Meeting Materials") in connection with its 2025 Annual General and Special Meeting of Shareholders to be held on May 8, 2025. The Meeting Materials are filed as exhibits to this Report on Form 6-K.
