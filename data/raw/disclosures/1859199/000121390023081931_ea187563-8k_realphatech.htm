Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

On November 1, 2023, reAlpha Tech Corp. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the commercial launch of GENA, formerly known as BnBGPT, an artificial intelligence-powered technology that develops, or enhances already existing, personalized listing descriptions for residential properties to be listed in online platforms, including Airbnb, Inc.’s platform, Zillow, VRBO and others. The Company had previously utilized GENA for internal use, and will now launch the technology for the public under the name GENA.

 

A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference herein.
