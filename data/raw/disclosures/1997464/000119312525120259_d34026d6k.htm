EXPLANATORY NOTE

Departure of Director

On May 13, 2025, Roger Nagioff submitted his resignation as a member of the Board of Directors of Marex Group plc (the “Company”), effective immediately. Mr. Nagioff represented private equity investors and was a member of the Company’s Risk Committee and its Remuneration Committee.

Withdrawal of Annual General Meeting Proposal

On April 2, 2025, the Company published its Notice of the 2025 Annual General Meeting, which included the proposals to be voted on by the Company’s shareholders. As a result of Mr. Nagioff’s departure, the Company intends to withdraw the resolution proposing his re-election as a director at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting (“AGM”). The withdrawal of this resolution does not otherwise affect the validity of the documentation issued by the Company in connection with the AGM or any votes already submitted on other resolutions. The numbering of all other proposed resolutions at the AGM remains unchanged.
