MATERIAL CHANGE – ANNOUNCEMENT OF CHANGE IN MANAGEMENT

 

On March 26, 2025, Mr. William Chai resigned from the Board of Directors of the Company and, on March 27, 2025, the Board of the Company accepted Mr. Chai’s resignation. The Board of Director further elected Mr. Marc Kealey as the Chairman of the Board and Mr. Jie Luo as the Vice Chairman of the Board. The Board of the Company announce that the former director Ms. Fan Zhou’s previous proposals regarding election of Mr. Jun Huang as a director and appointment of Mr. Jun Huang as the co-CEO are both invalid.
