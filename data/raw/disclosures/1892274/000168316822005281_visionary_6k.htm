Disposition of Subsidiaries

 

On July 26, 2022, our Board of Directors (the “Board”) decided to streamline our business lines, and focus on our core education sectors, including the operation of Ontario Secondary School Diploma, Griggs International Academy USA, Max the Mutt College for Animation, Design and Art, and future investment in education technology.

 

Therefore, the Board authorized the transfer of Visionary Study Abroad and Immigration Services Inc., Farvision Human Resource Service Company Inc., and The Princeton Career Education Group Inc. for a sum of $0.80 to their prior owner, Mr. Xiaofeng Wang. Mr. Wang had transferred these companies to us in 2021 for $0.80 and then worked for us until his departure in May 2022.
