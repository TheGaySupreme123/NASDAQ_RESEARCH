Item 8.01.   Other Events.

 

On November 22, 2022, Expion360 Inc. (the “Company”) received notice of a complaint (the “Complaint”) filed against it in Oregon state court by Ravi Sinha. The Complaint alleges, inter alia, that Mr. Sinha is entitled to 282,284 shares of the Company’s common stock in connection with services he previously rendered the Company as its chief executive officer. The Company believes that the Complaint is without merit and intends to contest the lawsuit vigorously.
