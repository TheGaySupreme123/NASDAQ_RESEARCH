On April 9, 2025, Mr. Boaz Shetzer provided notice of his intention to step down from his role as the Chief Executive Officer of ParaZero Technologies Ltd. (the “Company”) to pursue new opportunities, to be effective on July 8, 2025. Mr. Shetzer will continue to serve as the Company’s Chief Executive Officer during such notice period. Mr. Shetzer’s decision to step down did not arise or result from any disagreement with the Company.

 

This Form 6-K as Exhibit 99.1 is incorporated by reference into the Registrant’s Registration Statements on Form S-8 (File No. 333-278268 and 333-285054) and Form F-3 (File Nos. 333-281443 and 333-275351), filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission, to be a part thereof from the date on which this report is submitted, to the extent not superseded by documents or reports subsequently filed or furnished.
