INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS FORM 6-K REPORT

 

On January 28, 2026, the Company issued a statement to address inaccurate claims regarding its programs and operations. The Company confirmed that its research focuses on CAR γδ T cell and iPSC-derived γδ NKT cell therapies, and that its lead CAR γδ T cell program has received regulatory approval for a first-in-human clinical trial currently ongoing at National University Hospital, Singapore. CytoMed reaffirmed its commitment to regulatory compliance, scientific rigor, and transparent disclosure.
