INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS FORM 6-K REPORT

 

On August 28, 2025, CytoMed Therapeutics Limited (the “Company”) issued a press release, announcing that in addition to adult peripheral blood mononuclear cell-derived therapeutics, the Company has successfully expanded clinical-scale natural killer (“NK”) cells from cord blood units, and intends to re-organize and streamline its group structure to create a new cord blood-derived biotech.

 

A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report on Form 6-K.
