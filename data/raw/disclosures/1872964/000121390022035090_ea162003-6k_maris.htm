Results of the Special General Meeting of Shareholders 

 

On June 21, 2022, Maris-Tech Ltd. (the “Company”) convened a Special General Meeting of Shareholders (the “Meeting”).

 

The Meeting was called for the following purposes:

 

Proposal No. 1To approve the adoption of a compensation policy for the Company’s directors and officers;

 

Proposal No. 2To approve a fixed fee for Mr. Joseph Gottlieb, a member of the Company’s board of directors; and

 

Proposal No. 3To approve an update of the terms of compensation of and employment agreement with Mr. Israel Bar, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer and a member of the board of directors.

 

At the Meeting, a quorum was present and the shareholders of the Company approved all agenda items as originally proposed.

 

Compensation Policy

 

Attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 is the Company’s compensation policy, which reflects the approval of Proposal No. 1 above.
