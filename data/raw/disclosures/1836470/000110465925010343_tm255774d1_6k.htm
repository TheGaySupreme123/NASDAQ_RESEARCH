EXPLANATORY NOTE

 

On February 7, 2025, Sportradar Group AG (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that its subsidiary, Sportradar AG (“Sportradar”), entered into an eight-year exclusive license agreement (the “MLB Agreement”) through the 2032 MLB season with MLB Advanced Media, L.P. (“MLB”) and certain MLB affiliates. Pursuant to the MLB Agreement, Sportradar is MLB’s exclusive global distributor of betting and media data, as well as the exclusive distributor of live game video for international betting markets. Commencing with the 2025 season, Sportradar will also provide data distribution services and integrity services to MLB. In consideration of the rights and benefits granted under the MLB Agreement, Sportradar has agreed to pay MLB the applicable annual license fees and the parties entered into a subscription agreement pursuant to which MLB will be issued up to an aggregate of 1,855,724 Class A ordinary shares of the Company over the duration of the MLB Agreement, subject to customary terms, conditions and adjustments. The press release attached to this Current Report on Form 6-K as Exhibit 99.1 is incorporated herein by reference.
