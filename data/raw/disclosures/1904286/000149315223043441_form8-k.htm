Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

 

Appointment of Director

 

On December 1, 2023, the Board of Directors of the Company unanimously approved the appointment of Mr. Michael Jerman as Chairman of the Audit Committee of the Board. Mr. Jerman had previously been appointed to serve on the Company’s Audit Committee on November 14, 2023.

 

Also, on December 1, 2023, Brian Daly resigned from the Board of Directors of the Company. At the time of Mr. Daly’s resignation, Mr. Daly served as the Chairperson of the Audit Committee, and a member of the Compensation Committee. Mr. Daly’s decision to resign did not involve disagreements with the Company, the Company’s management, or the Board of Directors on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies, or practices.
