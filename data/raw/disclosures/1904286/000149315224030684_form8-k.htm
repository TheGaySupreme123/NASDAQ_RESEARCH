Item 8.01 Other Events

 

On August 7, 2024, MIRA Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the “Company”) received notification from the Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) that the Company has regained compliance with Nasdaq’s continued listing requirements under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2) concerning the minimum bid price requirement. The Company has resolved all issues cited by Nasdaq and is now in good standing with all applicable requirements for continued listing on the Nasdaq.

 

The Company is pleased to have regained compliance and will continue to adhere to Nasdaq’s listing standards.
