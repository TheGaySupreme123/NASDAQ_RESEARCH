Item 8.01 Other Events

 

MIRA Pharmaceuticals Unveils Additional Preclinical Success

 

MIRA Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the “Company”) unveiled promising preclinical data on Ketamir-2’s principal metabolite, Nor-Ketamir – a longer acting, brain penetrating and selective compound acting on the NMDA Complex’s PCP Receptor.

 

The Company recently received the results of new preclinical testing on its innovative drug candidate Ketamir-2 and its primary metabolite Nor-Ketamir-2. The new data reveals that Nor-Ketamir-2 selectively interacts with the NMDA PCP site while avoiding activity on the glutamate NMDA site. Additionally, a new salt formulation, Ketamir-2 Pamoate, has been developed to enhance pharmacokinetics, achieving nearly 100% oral bioavailability and improved brain penetration.

 

The Company is actively progressing with its IND-enabling regulatory work and Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) drug scale-up. The Company’s plan is to submit an Investigational New Drug (IND) application by the end of the year, aiming to initiate clinical trials. These advancements highlight the commitment to developing safer and more effective treatments, moving closer to bringing Ketamir-2 to market.
