EXPLANATORY NOTE

 

Turbo Energy, S.A. (“Turbo Energy” or the “Company”) is filing this Report on Form 6-K to report the results of the Company’s Extraordinary General Shareholders’ Meeting (the “Meeting), held at its registered offices, Calle Isabel la Católica, 8 Oficinas 50-51, 46004 Valencia, Spain, on Wednesday, December 18, 2024 at 1:00 P.M. CET (UTC+01:00),

 

As of the record date, November 20 2024, the Company had 55,085,700 Ordinary Shares issued and outstanding. A total of 92.8%, or 51,177,950 shares, were voted and a quorum was achieved.
