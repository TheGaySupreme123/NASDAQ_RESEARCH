Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

On May 22, 2024, Sow Good Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”) entered into an industrial lease (the “Lease”) with USCIF Pinnacle Building B LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Lessor”). Pursuant to the terms of the Lease, the Company will lease approximately 324,000 rentable square feet from the Lessor at 4024 Rock Quarry Road, Dallas, Texas (the “Premises”) for a term of approximately 62 months (the “Term”), which the Company intends to use as industrial and manufacturing space. The Term of the Lease commenced on May 22, 2024 (the “Lease Commencement Date”). The Lease provides for graduated rent payments starting at $122,175 per month, and increasing up to $297,289.14 per month by the end of the Lease, plus taxes, insurance and common area maintenance costs. The Company is required to provide a security deposit in the amount of $1,000,000 in connection with the Lease. The Lease may be renewed upon the extension in writing between the Company and the Lessor for a period of up to 60 months.

 

The foregoing summary of the Lease does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Lease, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
