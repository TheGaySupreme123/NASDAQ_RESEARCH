Item 8.01. Other Events.

On May 9, 2024 the underwriters of the recently completed public offering of common stock of Sow Good Inc. (the “Company”) purchased 180,000 shares of common stock (“Additional Shares”) pursuant to the full exercise of their option to purchase additional shares of common stock to cover over-allotments at the initial offering price of $10.00 per share. Including proceeds from the Additional Shares, the aggregate gross proceeds from the public offering were approximately $13.8 million before offering expenses and underwriting discounts and commissions.

 

On May 9, 2024, the Company issued a press release announcing the issuance of the Additional Shares. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.

 

The information furnished pursuant to this Item 8.01, including Exhibit 99.1, shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, and shall not be deemed to be incorporated by reference into any filing of the Company under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
