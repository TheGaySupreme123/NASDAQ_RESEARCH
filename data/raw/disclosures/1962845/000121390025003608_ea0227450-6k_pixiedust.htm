INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS REPORT ON FORM 6-K

 

On January 15, 2025, Pixie Dust Technologies, Inc. (the “Company”) resolved to issue two (2) Class D1 Preferred Shares (“D1 Shares”), by allotting one (1) D1 Share to Suzuyo & Co., Ltd. (“Suzuyo”) and one (1) D1 Share to Suzuyo Shoji Co., Ltd. (“Suzuyo Shoji”), for a total subscription amount of 1.5 billion yen.

 

A copy of the press release announcing this issuance is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 to this report on Form 6-K.

 

The information in this report on Form 6-K, including Exhibit 99.1 attached hereto, is being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for the purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any of the Company’s filings under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, whether made before or after the date hereof, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference to this report in such filing.
