Proxy Materials for Special General Meeting

 

Annexed hereto and incorporated by reference herein are copies of the following documents that are being distributed by the board of directors of Jeffs’ Brands Ltd (“Jeffs’ Brands” or the “Company”) to Jeffs’ Brands’ shareholders in connection with a Special General Meeting of Shareholders of the Company (the “Meeting”), which is scheduled to be held at 12 p.m. local (Israel) time on Monday, April 28, 2025 at the Company’s principal executive offices, which are located at 7 Mezada St. Bnei Brak, 5126112 Israel.

 

(i)Notice and Proxy Statement, each dated April 7, 2025, describing the proposals to be presented at, and logistical information related to, the Meeting (Exhibit 99.1); and
