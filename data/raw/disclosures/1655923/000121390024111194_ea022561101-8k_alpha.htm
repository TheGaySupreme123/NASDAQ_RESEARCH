Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities. 

 

On December 16, 2024, pursuant to the underwriting agreement (the “Underwriting Agreement”) by and among Alpha Cognition Inc. (the “Company”) Titan Partners Group LLC, a division of American Capital Partners, LLC, as representative (the “Representative”) of the several underwriters identified on Schedule A of the Underwriting Agreement (the “Underwriters”), the Company issued a representative’s purchase warrant (the “Representative’s Purchase Warrant”) to purchase up to an aggregate of 34,196 shares of Common Stock. The Representative’s Purchase Warrant may be exercised beginning on June 14, 2025 until November 8, 2029. The initial exercise price of each Underwriters’ Warrant is $7.18 per share. The issuance of the Representative’s Purchase Warrant was made pursuant to the exemption from registration contained in Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended.
