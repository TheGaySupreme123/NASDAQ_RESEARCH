Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

 

Convertible Promissory Note

 

On December 20, 2024, Powell Max Limited, a business company incorporated under the laws of the British Virgin Islands (the “Company”), issued a convertible promissory note (the “Note”) to YA II PN, Ltd. (the “Investor”) in the principal amount of $1,000,000 at an original issuance discount of 8% pursuant to Standby Equity Purchase Agreement, dated as of November 21, 2024 (the “SEPA”), the terms of which were previously disclosed by the Company’s in its Current Report on Form 6-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on November 22, 2024. The Note represents the third Pre-Advance under the SEPA.
