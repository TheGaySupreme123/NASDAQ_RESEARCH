Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

On May 23, 2025, Powell Max Limited, a business company incorporated under the laws of the British Virgin Islands (the “Company”) and YA II PN Ltd. (the “Investor”) entered into a Termination Agreement pursuant to which the Standby Equity Purchase Agreement, dated as of November 21, 2024 between the Company and the Investor and the Registration Rights Agreement, dated as of November 21, 2024 between the Company and the Investor were terminated. Each party has agreed to waive any claims arising under the two agreements prior to termination and to terminate the provisions thereunder, including certain provisions that by their terms would otherwise survive after termination. The Termination Agreement is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1.
