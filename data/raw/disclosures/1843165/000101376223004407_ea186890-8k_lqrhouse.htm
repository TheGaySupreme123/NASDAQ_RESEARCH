Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

On October 15, 2023, LQR House Inc. (the “Company” or “LQR”) entered into a services agreement (the “Services Agreement”) with X-Media Inc. (“X-Media”) pursuant to which the Company engaged X-Media to provide e-commerce services and website development services to the Company for the purposes of marketing of a new alcoholic beverage product (the “Services”). In consideration for the Services, the Company paid X-Media a cash fee of USD$2,400,000 upon signing the Services Agreement. The Services Agreement has an initial term of two (2) years.

 

The foregoing is a summary of certain material terms and conditions of the Services Agreement and is not a complete description thereof. Accordingly, the foregoing is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Services Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 which is incorporated herein by reference.
