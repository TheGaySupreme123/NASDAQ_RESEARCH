Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

On October 27, 2023, LQR House Inc. (the “Company” or “LQR”) entered into a consulting agreement (the “Consulting Agreement”) with IR Agency LLC (“IR Agency”) pursuant to which the Company engaged IR Agency to provide consulting services to the Company related to preparing a marketing program for the Company with a tentative start date of November 1, 2023 (the “Services”). In consideration for the Services, the Company paid IR Agency a nonrefundable cash fee of USD$1,000,000 upon signing the Consulting Agreement. The Consulting Agreement may be terminated by either party at any time.

 

The foregoing summary does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by the Consulting Agreement, copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
