Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities

 

On June 8, 2023, Connexa Sports Technologies Inc. (the “Company”) issued the following shares of its common stock (“Shares”) to the following persons in transactions that were exempt from registration under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), pursuant to Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act as transactions by an issuer not involving any public offering:

 

  1. 1,737,442 Shares to a lender in connection with the conversion of the outstanding principal amount of a $1,000,000 2.25% Promissory Note due April 30, 2021 into shares of common stock of the Company in exchange for a sufficient amount of shares of the Company to realize $1,500,000 in proceeds from the sale of shares of the Company’s common stock. A previous tranche of 272,332 shares of the Company’s common stock was issued to this lender in 2021 and one or more subsequent tranches of common stock of the Company may be required to be issued in order for the lender to realize $1,500,000 in aggregate proceeds; and
  2. 1,500 Shares to an ambassador as compensation to such ambassadors under their ambassador agreements.
