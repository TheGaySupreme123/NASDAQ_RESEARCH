Item 8.01 Other Events.

 

On March 24, 2022, Sunshine Biopharma, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into amendments to the warrants issued in the Company’s private placement that closed March 14, 2022, with the holders of such warrants. Pursuant to the amendments, the minimum exercise price of the warrants that such exercise price may be reduced to in the event of certain subsequent equity sales by the Company was increased from $2.00 to $2.09. In addition, the minimum price for certain subsequent equity sales by the Company that would not require the consent of holders of 70% of the outstanding warrants, was increased from $2.00 to $2.09.
