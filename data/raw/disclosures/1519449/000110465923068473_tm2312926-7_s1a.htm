EXPLANATORY NOTE
Skyward Specialty Insurance Group, Inc. is filing this Amendment No. 1 (this “Amendment”) to its Registration Statement on Form S-1 (Registration Statement No. 333-272427) (the “Registration Statement”) to revise Part II, Item 13. The remainder of the Registration Statement is unchanged and therefore has not been included in this Amendment.
