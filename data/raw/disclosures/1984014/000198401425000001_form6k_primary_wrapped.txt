PIPE Financing Transaction

BloomZ Inc. (the "Company") is furnishing this Report on Form 6-K to disclose
that on April 22, 2025, it entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement with
several non-U.S. investors. Pursuant to the Agreement, the Company successfully
raised approximately US$14,000,000 through a private placement (PIPE)
transaction conducted in reliance on Regulation S under the Securities Act of
1933, as amended.
