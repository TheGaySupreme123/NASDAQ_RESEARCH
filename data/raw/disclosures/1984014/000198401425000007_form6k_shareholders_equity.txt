Shareholders Equity Update in Accordance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(b)(1)

BloomZ Inc. the Company hereby reports that as of May 20, 2025, its
shareholders equity has exceeded US$5 million, in compliance with the
minimum continued listing requirement under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(b)(1).

The shareholders equity position as of the reporting date is detailed below:

Balance as of March 31, 2025: USD 1,839,683

Capital raised from April 1 to May 20, 2025: USD 3,275,405

Total Shareholders Equity as of May 20, 2025: USD 5,115,088

This equity increase is the result of successful private placement financings
and strategic capital infusions in recent months. The Company remains committed
 to fulfilling all Nasdaq continued listing standards.
