On February 7, 2025, Fitell Corporation, a Cayman Islands company (the “Company”), issued a press release announcing that it has entered into a definitive agreement with an institutional investor for the issuance and sale in a registered direct offering of an aggregate of 1,992,032 ordinary shares, par value $0.0001 per share (the “Ordinary Shares”) and warrants to purchase up to 1,992,032 Ordinary Shares, at a purchase price of $5.02 per Ordinary Share and associated warrants. The offering is expected to close on or about February 10, 2025, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. A copy of such press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Report on Form 6-K and incorporated herein by reference.

 

Neither this Report on Form 6-K, nor the exhibit attached hereto, is an offer to sell or the solicitation of an offer to buy the securities described herein or therein.
