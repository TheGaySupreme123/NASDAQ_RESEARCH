INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS FORM 6-K REPORT

 

On March 20, 2023, Bruush Oral Care Inc. (the “Company”) closed its issuance of an unsecured promissory note in a principal amount of US$2,749,412 (the “Note”) to a single investor. The Note was issued at an original issue discount of 15% and matures on July 18, 2023. The Note contains customary and standard representations and warranties, covenants, and events of default. The Company intends to use the net proceeds of $2,337,000 for working capital, growth capital, and other general corporate purposes.

 

The description of the Note set forth above is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Note, which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1.
