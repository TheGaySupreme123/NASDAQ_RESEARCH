EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Amendment No. 1 to our Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended December 31, 2024 (the “Original Filing”), filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on April 25, 2025, is being filed for the sole purpose of amending and restating in its entirety “Item 19. Exhibits” under Part III of the Original Filing to include our Compensation Clawback Policy. Except as noted above, this Form 20-F (Amendment No. 1) does not update or modify any disclosures in or reflect any events occurring after the Original Filing. Accordingly, this Form 20-F (Amendment No. 1) should be read in conjunction with the Original Filing.
