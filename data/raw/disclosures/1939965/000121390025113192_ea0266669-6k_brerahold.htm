INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS FORM 6-K REPORT

 

Press Release

 

On November 20, 2025, Brera Holdings PLC, operating under the name Solmate Infrastructure (the “Company”) (NASDAQ: SLMT), a Solana-based crypto infrastructure company, issued a press release announcing the Company will serve as an official Strategic Partner of Abu Dhabi Finance Week, taking place 8-11 December 2025. A copy of the press release is attached to this Form 6-K as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.

 

PIPE Investor Notice

 

In connection with the filing of the resale registration statement on Form F-3ASR (Registration Number 333-291657) by the Company, the Company is concurrently sending notice to the PIPE Investors (as defined below) as required by the Registration Rights Agreement, dated as of September 18, 2025, by and between the Company and certain accredited investors (the “PIPE Investors”). The notice includes certain business updates. A copy of the notice is attached to this Form 6-K as Exhibit 99.2 and is incorporated herein by reference.
