EXPLANATORY NOTE
On March 17, 2025, IREN Limited (the “Company”) released a press release announcing the signing of a 600MW grid connection agreement with AEP Texas Inc. in relation to its Sweetwater 2 project in West Texas.
The Company expects to pay $4.1 million in non-refundable connection costs and $26.9 million in refundable deposits over the next 12 months. The project has an expected energization date in late 2027.
A copy of the Company’s press release is furnished hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
