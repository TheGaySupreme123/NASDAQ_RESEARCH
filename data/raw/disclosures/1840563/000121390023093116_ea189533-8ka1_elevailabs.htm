Explanatory Note

 

On December 4, 2023, Elevai Labs Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Elevai”), filed a Current Report on Form 8-K (the “Original Form 8-K”) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”). This amendment to Form 8-K corrects an omission to the Index to Exhibits in Item 9.01 of the Original Form 8-K and to file Exhibit 10.1, which as disclosed in the Original Form 8-K was to be filed with the Company’s quarterly report on form 10-Q. This Form 8-K/A does not amend or update any other item or disclosure contained in the Original Form 8-K. This Form 8-K/A is presented as of the filing date of the Original Form 8-K and does not reflect events occurring after that date, or modify or update disclosures in any way other than as specifically noted above.
