Expansion of Hanryu

Korean culture (Hanryu) is growing rapidly with the spread of art, music and entertainment. We aim at uniting global Hanryu fans with FANTOO based on the growth of Hanryu.

The Meet of FANTOO, Hanryu and the World

We will enrich user’s fandom experience by providing an all-in-one platform for fandom content, including news, popular culture, discussions, live shows, and fan creativity.

The Start of FANTOO
