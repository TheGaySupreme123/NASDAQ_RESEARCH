Corporate Profile

We are a life sciences instrumentation and research services company engaged in the development of scientific assets and novel intellectual property across multiple “omics” fields. Our mission is to leverage our expertise with True Single Molecule Sequencing ("tSMS") technology to enable researchers and clinicians to contribute major advancements to scientific research and development by accelerating one’s understanding of the molecular mechanisms of disease and fundamental biological processes.
