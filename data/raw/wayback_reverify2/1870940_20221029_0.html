FAQ'S

What is AirSculpt?

AirSculpt® is patented, precision-engineered technology only available at Elite Body Sculpture. This treatment removes unwanted fat through a freckle-sized hole cell by cell with no needle, no scalpel, and no stitches – all while patients remain wide awake. This revolutionary technology also leaves the skin tighter than before, providing dramatic, smooth, and natural results.

What areas can I treat with AirSculpt?

We like to say, “if you can pinch it, we can take it,” as you can AirSculpt® pretty much anywhere between your chin and ankles. The general areas we treat include the chin, abdomen, waist, love handles, back, legs, arms, banana rolls, and pubic region. We also offer AirSculpt fat transfers to the hands, breasts, buttocks, and hips. Click on the procedure tab on our website for a complete list of treatment areas, and be sure to check out the thousands of before and after photos we have listed and accompanying AirSculpt reviews from satisfied patients.

Are there weight/ BMI requirements?

There is no specific BMI or weight requirement for AirSculpt®. 

Can AirSculpt damage any organs?

AirSculpt® only targets subcutaneous fat, the pinchable layer just beneath the skin. Our automated technology is designed to turn off before coming into contact with deeper layers like visceral fat. 

Does AirSculpt look unnatural?

No, AirSculpt does not look unnatural. Our primary objective is to deliver results that accentuate the body’s natural contours with no sign a procedure was done whatsoever.

Why is AirSculpt less invasive?

In lieu of the scalpel and surgical incision used in liposuction, the AirSculpt® process enters delicately and relies on our patented spinning technology to gently pluck out your fat through an entry site smaller than a pencil eraser. As the treated area heals, only a mark similar to a freckle or natural skin blemish is left behind; there’s no surgical scar with tell-tale suture marks. Patients recover far more quickly than traditional liposuction, and since there are no stitches, there’s no need for a follow-up surgeon’s visit to have them removed.

Does this procedure work for all age groups?

Yes, AirSculpt works on patients of any age as long as they are over 18.

Is any diet necessary before AirSculpt?

​​No diet is necessary before AirSculpt. 

Can AirSculpt tighten my loose skin?

AirSculpt does provide minimal skin tightening during the procedure, but if loose skin is your main concern, you might want to consider other options like a Tummy Tuck or a lift. If you are still looking for a fat removal procedure and take care of your loose skin, you can always do our AirSculpt procedure first and take care of your loose skin after recovery if need be.

Will my cellulite be treated or affected?

Some patients may experience a degree of improvement afterward, but AirSculpt lipo is not a designated cellulite treatment.
