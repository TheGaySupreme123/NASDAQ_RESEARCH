ABOUT AGAPE ATP

We Are Agape ATP Corporation

AGAPE ATP Corporation (Nasdaq: ATPC) stands as a beacon of innovation and commitment to holistic well-being since its inception in 2016. Founded by the visionary Dr. How Kok Choong, our company is dedicated to revolutionizing health and wellness solutions and focusing on renewable energy solutions to address the evolving challenges of modern living.

Through relentless research and development efforts, combined with a deep understanding of market needs, we have consistently pushed the boundaries of what's possible in enhancing Holistic Health and Wellness. Our journey reached a significant milestone in 2023 with our successful listing on NASDAQ (Nasdaq: ATPC), a testament to our unwavering dedication to innovation and excellence.

Now, as we look towards the future, our renewable energy solutions division is a strategic move which empowers our commitment to sustainability and our recognition of the urgent need to address environmental challenges.

By leveraging our expertise in scientific and technological advancements, coupled with strategic partnerships with global industry leaders, we aim to build a comprehensive renewable energy ecosystem. This ecosystem will not only contribute to mitigating the impact of climate change but also aligns seamlessly with our mission to improve the well-being of individuals and communities worldwide.

More Infomation

At Agape ATP, we believe that true wellness encompasses not only physical health but also environmental stewardship. Our venture into renewable energy solutions is a natural extension of our commitment to creating a healthier, more sustainable future for generations to come.

As we embark on this exciting new chapter, we remain steadfast in our dedication to innovation, excellence, and the well-being of our clients. Together, we will harness the power of science and technology to build a brighter, more sustainable tomorrow.
