Corporate Profile

Lucy Scientific Discovery is an early-stage psychotropics manufacturing company focused on becoming the premier contract research, development, and manufacturing organization for the emerging psychotropics-based medicines industry. Our mission is to make our products and research services available for the development of medicines and experimental therapies to address certain psychiatric health disorders and other medical needs including various mental health and addiction disorders. We further generate meaningful revenue concurrently with the blossoming of the psychedelic medicinal substances space by providing the consumer goods market with commercial products that are not subject to strict regulatory controls associated with pharmaceuticals. Our executive team brings deep experience in the development and commercialization of products featuring controlled substances as well as the navigation of regulatory structures applicable to these products. Lucy Scientific Discovery trades on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol LSDI.
