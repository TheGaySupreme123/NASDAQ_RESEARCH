INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS

DRIVEN BY EVs
 

Positioned to capitalize on the rapid growth in demand for lithium, driven by the electrification of the global automobile fleet.

EXPLORATION POTENTIAL

Only 1% of the nearly 60,000-acre site of the site has been explored and has uncovered 11.1M metric tonnes indicated and inferred resource at 1% Li2O

GEO PROXIMITY
 

Geographic proximity to the expanding North American electric vehicle and Sustainable Critical minerals production positions us as an integral provider of sustainable raw materials.

160,000 TONNES/YEAR
 

Based on current resource, a fully functioning lithium mine could produce 160,000 tonnes per annum of 6% lithium spodumene concentrate over an 8-10 year period.

EXISTING RESOURCE
 

The Project already contains a SK-1300 11.1Mt indicated and inferred resource at 1%Li20 with further resource updates pending

EXPERIENCED TEAM
 

Leadership team of experienced mining executives and operators, with a track record of de-risking and delivering.
