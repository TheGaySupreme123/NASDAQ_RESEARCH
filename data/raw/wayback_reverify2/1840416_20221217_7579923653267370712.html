Solar modules and solar kits (Complete kits for retrofitting)

Our solar panels for vehicle use are available in eight different standard sizes (70 W – 500 W) and are suitable for roof mounting on a wide variety of vehicle types. Our solar kits are our all-in-one solution for standard vehicle types, from lorries to city buses.

Depending on your requirements, our kits include standard-size solar panels, ready for installation, 12 V / 24 V Sono Solar charge controller, installation kit and cables and the Sono Solar dashboard and telematics unit.

Solar modules for use in vehicles made to measure

As your development partner, we make every roof surface usable. We tailor solar modules to your requirements in terms of size, electrical specifications and design. Quickly and easily.

All our solar modules are tested for use in vehicles and are perfectly matched to our in-house solar charge controllers, ensuring maximum performance even in partial shade.

Our Partners and Customer Projects

Amongst our customers and partners are some of the largest vehicle and equipment manufacturers – from the Truck, Trailer and Bus / Coach industry. We are proud to work on pioneering projects on several different vehicle types. Learn more about some of our delivered customer projects and partnerships:

Since 2025, MAN Truck and Bus S.E. offers a SonoSolar Solution as an accessory for their Lion’s Coach Series – being delivered and pre-installed in the production line. With that step, SonoSolar and MAN push Solar Integration to the next level. Bus operators benefit from this convenience – and passengers profit from enhanced travel comfort. You want to learn more about the key benefits of solar integration for buses and coach travel?

 

We have realized several refrigerated trailer pilots with European Trailer Manufacturers over the last years, which are driving across Europe and collect valuable data about Solar Yield and its impact on cooling performance and energy independence. Our High automotive Solar Modules provide free-of-charge and clean solar energy to cool the transported goods – reducing the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), cut Diesel Costs and reduce charging time. Cooled by the Sun.

We are collaborating on refrigerated trailer pilots together with several TRU (Transport Refrigeration Unit) manufacturers. With those, we have realized electrical interfaces between our in-house developed High Voltage Solar Charge Controllers and their electrical or hybrid powered TRUs. That means, the SonoSolar Solution for Refrigerated Trailers and Trucks is compatible with most TRUs in the market. 

Since early 2025 we have collaborated in a close partnership with Merlin Solar Technologies from the US. Merlin Solar will distribute Sono’s solar charge controller technology as part of its integrated power systems in North and South America, leveraging its established network and engineering capabilities.

SonoSolar in turn, will distribute Merlin’s cutting-edge solar modules throughout Europe, ensuring access to high-efficiency PV hardware that complements its existing solutions.

 

 

We collaborate with global commercial vehicle OEMs – for example as part of the EU-funded SolarMoves project with Ford Motor Company and their Ford Pro Special Vehicles team – to explore the real-world potential of Vehicle Integrated Photovoltaics (VIPV).

Together, we’re currently testing solar integration on a Ford E-Transit, equipped with a 1 kWp Solar Installation and our High Voltage (HV) Solar Charge Controller. The controller optimizes the energy yield of each panel and converts low-voltage solar power to the high-voltage level of the vehicle’s traction battery.

We equipped several city and overland buses – electric, hybrid and diesel with our Solar Solution, all over Europe. As one of the most outstanding projects, we integrated solar on a small fleet of Diesel Overland Buses with Scania, a Volkswagen Subsidiary. These buses drive in the City of Lulea in Northern Sweden, proving the high potential, solar integration on buses even has in Northern Europe.
