Argo Blockchain PLC is a global data centre business that provides a powerful and efficient platform for cryptocurrency mining operations.

The company is headquartered in London, UK, with operations in strategic locations in North America, and its shares are listed on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange under the ticker: ARB and on NASDAQ Global Select Market in the United States under the ticker ARBK.
